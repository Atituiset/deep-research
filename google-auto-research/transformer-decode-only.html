0. Historical Context: From "Specialist" to "Generalist"

2017

Transformer 诞生：机器翻译的 Seq2Seq

Transformer Born: Seq2Seq for Translation

最初是为了替代 RNN 做机器翻译。Encoder 负责看懂源语言（如英语），Decoder 负责生成目标语言（如法语）。此时依然是针对特定任务设计的结构。

Originally built to replace RNNs for translation. Encoder reads source language, Decoder generates target language. Still a task-specific design.

2018 - 2019

BERT 时代：Encoder 预训练主导

The BERT Era: Encoder Pre-training Dominates

Google 推出 BERT，抛弃 Decoder，仅用 Encoder 做“完形填空”。在文本分类、阅读理解等任务上刷新榜单。AI 界普遍认为“双向理解”才是王道。

Google launched BERT, dropping Decoder, using Encoder for "Fill-in-the-blank". It dominated classification tasks. The industry believed "Bidirectional understanding" was king.

2020 - 至今

GPT-3 与大一统：纯 Decoder 吞噬一切

GPT-3 & Unification: Decoder Devours All

OpenAI GPT-3 证明了只要规模够大，通过纯 Decoder 的“单向文字接龙”，模型就能学会所有的任务。我们不再需要为了不同任务调整模型结构。

OpenAI's GPT-3 proved at scale, pure Decoder's "word continuation" learns all tasks. We no longer tweak model architectures for different tasks.

Bidirectional Attention

能够同时看到 Token 的上下文（通过 Masked Language Modeling 训练）。非常适合做文本分类、命名实体识别（NER）等需要**全局视角**的任务。但在开放式长文本生成上效率低下，存在训练和推理的差异。

GPT 系列 (Decoder-Only)

Causal Masking

Strictly follows causality; current token only sees past tokens. Trained via **Next Token Prediction**. Natively suited for Autoregressive generation, seamlessly appending generated tokens to context.

0. 历史脉络：从目标函数分歧到机理涌现

0. Historical Context: From Objective Divergence to Emergent Mechanics

2017 - 2018

拓扑原点：混合注意力的 Seq2Seq

Topological Origin: Mixed Attention Seq2Seq

Transformer 论文提出时，其本质是一个针对条件概率建模的模型。内部包含了双向自注意力、因果掩码自注意力以及交叉注意力三种不同的信息路由机制。

The original Transformer was built for conditional probability modeling. It contained three distinct routing mechanisms: bidirectional self-attention, causal masked self-attention, and cross-attention.

2019 - 2021

目标函数分歧：MLM 去噪 vs AR 似然估计

Objective Divergence: MLM Denoising vs AR Likelihood

学术界分裂为两派。Google 主推 Masked Language Modeling (如 BERT/T5)，强调双向去噪；而 OpenAI 坚持 Autoregressive 极大似然估计。随后 Dong et al. (2021) 从数学上证明了纯双向注意力的“秩崩塌”灾难。

Academia split: Google pushed Masked Language Modeling (BERT/T5) for bidirectional denoising; OpenAI persisted with Autoregressive Max Likelihood. Later, Dong et al. (2021) mathematically proved pure bidirectional attention suffers "Rank Collapse".

2022 - 至今

机理解密：感应头 (Induction Heads) 的发现

Mechanistic Decoding: Discovery of Induction Heads

Anthropic 发表突破性研究，证明 ICL 泛化能力的涌现高度依赖于模型内部形成的“感应头”神经回路。而严苛的 AR 目标（因果掩码阻断捷径）是迫使网络发育出这种回路的必要条件，从机理层面宣判了 Encoder 的死刑。

Anthropic's breakthrough proved ICL emergence depends heavily on "Induction Head" neural circuits. The strict AR objective (causal masking blocking shortcuts) is essential to force this growth, mechanistically ruling out Encoders.

1. 数学本性：双指数秩崩塌 (Rank Collapse)

1. Mathematical Nature: Double-Exponential Rank Collapse

为什么早期模型 (如 BERT) 通常只有 12-24 层，而现在的 Decoder 动辄 100 层以上？答案隐藏在注意力矩阵的谱特性（Spectral properties）中。Google 研究团队在 2021 年证明了纯双向注意力网络的致命缺陷：

Why were early models (like BERT) capped at 12-24 layers, while modern Decoders easily exceed 100 layers? The answer lies in the spectral properties of the attention matrix. Google researchers proved the fatal flaw of pure bidirectional attention:

定理：纯无跳跃连接注意力网络的输出趋于秩-1 矩阵

Theorem: Output of pure attention network without skip connections converges to rank-1

$$ \| \text{Attn}(X) - \mathbf{1}c^\top \| \le \mathcal{O}(e^{-2^L}) $$

Encoder 的退化

Encoder Degradation

在双向注意力中，每个 Token 都能看到全局。随着网络加深 ($L$ 增大)，注意力矩阵会以**双指数级 ($e^{-2^L}$)** 的速度退化，所有 Token 的表征会坍缩到一个低维子空间，失去区分度。

In bidirectional attention, every token sees the global context. As depth ($L$) increases, the attention matrix degrades at a **double-exponential ($e^{-2^L}$)** rate, causing all token representations to collapse into a rank-1 subspace, losing distinguishability.

Causal Mask 的救赎

Salvation via Causal Mask

Decoder 的下三角掩码天然打破了图的对称性。由于后面的 Token 不能影响前面的 Token，状态转移矩阵具有强特征根隔离，保证了矩阵始终满秩。这就是为什么 GPT 模型可以无限堆叠层数而不崩溃的数学基石。

The lower-triangular causal mask of Decoder naturally breaks graph symmetry. Since future tokens cannot influence past ones, the transition matrix maintains strong eigenvalue isolation, preserving full rank. This is the mathematical bedrock allowing GPT models to scale infinitely in depth.

2. 机械可解释性：捷径效应与感应头 (Induction Heads)

2. Mechanistic Interpretability: Shortcuts and Induction Heads

Anthropic 团队在机械可解释性研究中发现，In-Context Learning (ICL) 能力的涌现高度依赖于网络内部形成一种特殊的神经回路——感应头（Induction Heads）。这种回路负责寻找历史模式 [A][B] ... [A] -> 预测[B]。

Anthropic's Mechanistic Interpretability research revealed that In-Context Learning (ICL) emergence heavily depends on a specific neural circuit forming inside the network—Induction Heads. These circuits find historical patterns like [A][B] ... [A] -> predict[B].

  • Encoder 的“作弊”： 如果允许前向视野（双向注意力），网络在预测掩码词时，更倾向于通过局部句法结构走捷径（Shortcut Learning），而懒得发育出长距离模式匹配的“感应头”回路。
  • Decoder 的“逆境生存”： 严格的因果掩码阻断了捷径。模型为了降低极端的自回归交叉熵损失，被迫在训练早期经历一次相变（Phase Transition），大规模发育感应头回路，从而涌现出强大的上下文泛化能力。
  • Encoder's "Cheating": Given forward vision (bidirectional attention), the network prefers shortcuts via local syntax to predict masked words, failing to develop long-range pattern-matching induction heads.
  • Decoder's "Survival": Strict causal masking blocks shortcuts. To minimize extreme AR cross-entropy loss, the model is forced through a Phase Transition early in training, developing massive induction heads, yielding emergent ICL capabilities.

0. 历史脉络：系统工程视角的瓶颈转移

0. Historical Context: Bottleneck Shifts in Systems Engineering

2017 - 2019

算力计算密集时代 (Compute-Bound Era)

Compute-Bound Era

模型参数量通常在 100M-1B 级别，推理的瓶颈主要是矩阵乘法 (GEMM) 的 FLOPs 计算力。此时 Encoder-Decoder 架构的双向注意力带来的计算开销在系统层面是完全可接受的。

Model sizes were around 100M-1B. The primary inference bottleneck was GEMM FLOPs. At this scale, the extra compute overhead of Encoder-Decoder's bidirectional and cross-attention was acceptable.

2020 - 2022

撞上显存墙 (The Memory Wall Crisis)

Hitting the Memory Wall

随着模型扩展到 100B+（如 Megatron, PaLM），长序列生成时，大量的 KV 张量读写使系统迅速从 Compute-bound 变为 Memory-bound（访存受限）。异构的 Enc-Dec 导致显存碎片化严重，Batch Size 无法扩大。

As models scaled to 100B+, heavy KV tensor I/O during long-sequence generation shifted systems to being Memory-bound. Heterogeneous Enc-Dec caused severe VRAM fragmentation, capping batch sizes.

2023 - 至今

Serving 系统革命：同构生态的垄断

Serving Revolution: Monopoly of Homogeneous Ecosystems

vLLM (PagedAttention) 和 FlashAttention 横空出世。这些极致压榨底层 IO 的技术几乎全都是基于同构连续序列（即纯 Decoder）设计和优化的。Encoder 彻底失去了系统级基建的生态红利支持。

vLLM (PagedAttention) and FlashAttention emerged. These IO-maximizing technologies were designed and optimized almost exclusively for homogeneous continuous sequences (pure Decoder). Encoders lost ecosystem infra support.

1. 极致系统工程：Serving 吞吐与内存池化

1. Systems Engineering: Serving Throughput & Memory Pooling

在工业级 LLM 部署中，瓶颈早已从 FLOPs 转移到了访存带宽和显存碎片化管理上。vLLM 引入的 PagedAttention 和 Continuous Batching（连续批处理） 彻底改变了游戏规则，暴露了 Enc-Dec 架构的阿喀琉斯之踵。

In industrial LLM deployment, bottlenecks shifted from FLOPs to Memory Bandwidth and VRAM fragmentation. PagedAttention and Continuous Batching changed the rules, exposing Enc-Dec's Achilles' heel.

Enc-Dec 的异构上下文灾难

Enc-Dec Heterogeneous Disaster

在连续批处理中，调度器混合 Prefill 和 Decode 请求。Enc-Dec 在 Decode 时需要维持对 Encoder 的 Cross-Attention。内存中不仅要有自回归的 KV Cache，还要单独维护形状各异的 Encoder KV 张量，导致 Block 内存池管理极为困难，引发严重碎片。

Schedulers mix Prefill/Decode requests. Enc-Dec requires maintaining Cross-Attention to Encoder outputs. Memory must hold structurally heterogeneous Encoder KV tensors alongside AR caches, making Block pool management difficult and causing fragmentation.

纯 Decoder 的同构抽象胜利

Decoder Homogeneous Victory

Decoder-only 将 Prompt (X) 和 Generation (Y) 在物理显存层面拉平为同构的 1D 连续 Token 流。这种完美的同构性使得 RadixAttention (前缀树 KV 共享) 成为可能。结合 FlashAttention-3 的 WGM 优化，硬件亲和性达到极致。

Decoder-only flattens Prompt and Generation into a homogeneous 1D continuous token stream. This perfect homogeneity enables RadixAttention (prefix-tree KV sharing). Combined with FlashAttention-3 WGM, hardware affinity peaks.

2. 隐式元学习：In-Context Learning 就是梯度下降

2. Implicit Meta-Learning: ICL is Gradient Descent

为什么纯 Decoder 展现出了更强的 ICL 能力？学术界（von Oswald et al., 2023）证明了：基于线性自注意力的前向传播，在数学上等价于在内部隐状态上执行隐式的梯度下降（Implicit SGD）。

Why superior ICL in pure Decoders? Research (von Oswald et al., 2023) proved: forward pass of linear self-attention is mathematically equivalent to implicit gradient descent (Implicit SGD) on internal hidden states.

在 Enc-Dec 中，Demonstrations 送入 Encoder，Query 送入 Decoder。物理双塔隔离阻止了注意力矩阵作为“元优化器”在连续状态空间中直接更新权重。而 Decoder 的因果掩码强迫网络将之前的样本编码到了 KV 缓存中，实时完成网络权重的微调更新。

In Enc-Dec, demonstrations go to Encoder, queries to Decoder. Physical separation prevents attention from acting as a unified "meta-optimizer". Decoder's causal mask forces encoding prior samples into KV cache, executing real-time fine-tuning within the forward pass.
