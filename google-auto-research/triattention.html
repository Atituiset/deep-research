1. 摘要 & 背景 1. Abstract & Motivation

随着以 DeepSeek-R1、Qwen3-8B-Instruct、Llama3-8B 为首的长链推理模型 (Long Reasoning LLMs) 的广泛普及，模型在单次调用中生成多达数万个 Token 的思维链 (Chain-of-Thought, CoT) 成为常态。这种强思维密集、长文本迭代的场景，导致 Transformer 的 Key-Value (KV) Cache 显存占用呈线性暴涨，极速触及硬件的“物理显存墙 (Memory Wall)”。

With the widespread adoption of Long Reasoning LLMs pioneered by DeepSeek-R1, Qwen3-8B-Instruct, and Llama3-8B, it has become common for models to generate Chain-of-Thought (CoT) sequences containing tens of thousands of tokens in a single call. This computationally intensive, long-context iterative scenario causes the Key-Value (KV) Cache memory footprint to skyrocket linearly, rapidly hitting the hardware's physical "Memory Wall".

此前，工业界常使用 H2O、SnapKV 或 Accumulative Attention Eviction 机制。其核心逻辑是在解码时观察哪些 KV Token 被近期的 Query 强烈关注，从而淘汰注意力权重极低的历史 Token。

Previously, the industry frequently utilized mechanisms like H2O, SnapKV, or Accumulative Attention Eviction. Their core logic relies on observing which historical KV Tokens are strongly attended to by recent Queries during decoding, thereby evicting tokens with extremely low attention weights.

🚨 传统 Post-RoPE 淘汰机制的物理崩塌： 🚨 The Physical Collapse of Traditional Post-RoPE Eviction:

旋转位置编码 (RoPE) 在生成时会将 Query 向量按照位置进行非线性旋转。由于 Query 不断随生成位置发生角度偏移，代表性 Query 的范围极其局限（仅能覆盖最近的约 25 个位置）。

Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE) non-linearly rotates the Query vectors according to their positions during generation. Because Queries constantly undergo angular shifts based on their generation position, the scope of a representative Query is extremely limited (only covering approximately the last 25 positions).

此时，通过 Post-RoPE query-key 点积计算出来的注意力打分，是高度位置依赖且极其瞬态的。对于在长逻辑推理中“当前不重要、但后文极其关键”的暗线物理 Token，传统方法会将其永久性误淘汰，这直接造成推理准确率“断崖式下跌”。

Consequently, attention scores calculated via Post-RoPE query-key dot products are highly position-dependent and extremely transient. For implicit physical Tokens that are "unimportant now, but critically vital later" in long logical reasoning, traditional methods will permanently and mistakenly evict them, causing a "cliff-like drop" in reasoning accuracy.

2. TriAttention 核心技术原理 2. Core Technical Principles of TriAttention

Insight 01 Q/K Concentration

Q/K 向量的高凝聚性

High Concentration of Q/K Vectors

TriAttention 提出，如果把视线转移到 Pre-RoPE 空间 (即未加载旋转位置向量前的自注意力查询与键空间)，会观察到一个惊人的自然现象： 无论是 Query 还是 Key，在不同的位置、不同的序列上下文输入下，均高度聚敛在一个恒定且不为零的“成核中心 (Mean Center)”周围。

这意味着，RoPE 旋转本身才是注意力震荡变化的主要推手。一旦排除 RoPE 的“万花筒效应”，注意力的概率密度就可以通过这个恒定中心结合两者的物理距离，进行直接的、代数级别的解耦和预测。

TriAttention posits that if we shift our focus to the Pre-RoPE space (the self-attention query and key space before rotary position vectors are loaded), an astonishing natural phenomenon is observed: Whether Query or Key, across different positions and varying sequence context inputs, they are highly concentrated around a constant, non-zero "Mean Center".

This implies that the RoPE rotation itself is the primary driver of attention oscillation. Once the "kaleidoscope effect" of RoPE is eliminated, the probability density of attention can be directly decoupled and algebraically predicted using this constant center combined with their physical distance.

Insight 02 Distance Preferences

可预测的位置/距离偏好

Predictable Distance Preferences

既然 Pre-RoPE 空间的 $Q$ 向量高度靠拢它的期望中心 $\expected{q}$，那么只要确定了当前 Key 向量 $k$ 所在的具体坐标，注意力的强度就不再依赖动态实时变化、反复计算的 Query，而是转为 $k$ 与 $\expected{q}$ 在旋转频率域内的相位偏好。

由于 RoPE 对每个复数维度 slice 存在着固定的频率步长 $\omega_f$，这种中心偏好便可严密写为一组关于 Query-Key 物理索引差值（即 $\Delta = p_q - p_k$）的三角级数之和 (Trigonometric Series Sum)。这也正是 TriAttention 名字中 "Tri" 的代数来源。

Since the $Q$ vectors in the Pre-RoPE space closely hug their expected center $\expected{q}$, as long as the specific coordinate of the current Key vector $k$ is determined, the attention intensity no longer relies on dynamically changing, repeatedly calculated Queries. It transitions into a phase preference between $k$ and $\expected{q}$ within the rotational frequency domain.

Because RoPE exhibits a fixed frequency step size $\omega_f$ for each complex dimension slice, this center preference can be rigorously written as a Trigonometric Series Sum regarding the physical index difference between Query and Key ($\Delta = p_q - p_k$). This is exactly the algebraic origin of the "Tri" in TriAttention's name.

3. 三角极值投影数学模型 3. Trigonometric Extrema Projection Math Model

TriAttention 的打分模块由两个子项构成：三角投影打分 ($S_{trig}$) 与 期望模长修正打分 ($S_{norm}$)。两者通过中心凝聚度测度 $R_f$ 自动平衡。

The scoring module of TriAttention consists of two sub-terms: Trigonometric Projection Score ($S_{trig}$) and Expected Norm Correction Score ($S_{norm}$). Both are automatically balanced via the central concentration measure $R_f$.

凝聚度测度：平均结果长度

Concentration Measure: Mean Resultant Length

定量衡量每个自注意力头（或频段子空间 $f$）在 Pre-RoPE 空间的聚敛度，公式如下：

Quantitatively measures the convergence of each self-attention head (or frequency band subspace $f$) in the Pre-RoPE space, formulated as:

$$R_f = \frac{\norm{\expected{q_f}}}{\expected{\norm{q_f}}}$$

其中 $q_f \in \mathbb{C}$ 为复平面的坐标，$\norm{\cdot}$ 为复数模。$R_f$ 取值于 $[0, 1]$；值越接近 1.0，说明 Query 在该频段的方差越小，凝聚性越强。

Where $q_f \in \mathbb{C}$ are coordinates on the complex plane, and $\norm{\cdot}$ denotes the complex modulus. $R_f$ ranges from $[0, 1]$; values closer to 1.0 indicate smaller variance of Queries in that frequency band, implying stronger concentration.

三角级数投影分量 ($S_{trig}$)

Trigonometric Series Projection Component ($S_{trig}$)

假设 Q 在期望中心 $\expected{q_f}$ 处完美坍缩。代入旋转变换，Key 的三角级数分量计算如下：

Assuming Q collapses perfectly at the expected center $\expected{q_f}$. By substituting the rotational transformation, the Key's trigonometric series component is calculated as:

$$S_{trig}(k, \Delta) = \sum_f \norm{\expected{q_f}} \cdot \norm{k_f} \cdot \cos(\omega_f \Delta + \phi_f)$$

其中 $\phi_f = \text{arg}(\expected{q_f}) - \text{arg}(k_f)$ 为期望查询质心与当前 Key 在 Pre-RoPE 复平面下的角度偏好角。$\omega_f$ 是频段 $f$ 的旋转速率（由 RoPE 旋转步长控制）。

Where $\phi_f = \text{arg}(\expected{q_f}) - \text{arg}(k_f)$ is the angular preference between the expected query centroid and the current Key in the Pre-RoPE complex plane. $\omega_f$ is the rotation rate of frequency band $f$ (controlled by the RoPE rotation step size).

期望模长补充项 ($S_{norm}$)

Expected Norm Supplement Term ($S_{norm}$)

为了兼容极少数未能展示出强凝聚性的注意力头 ($R_f \ll 1$)，引入以下不依赖绝对位置的修正项：

To accommodate the rare attention heads that fail to exhibit strong concentration ($R_f \ll 1$), the following position-independent correction term is introduced:

$$S_{norm}(k) = \sum_f (1 - R_f) \cdot \expected{\norm{q_f}} \cdot \norm{k_f}$$

等价展开后可写作 $\sum_f (\expected{\norm{q_f}} - \norm{\expected{q_f}}) \cdot \norm{k_f}$。对于高凝聚频段，$1 - R_f \to 0$ 使其几乎不参与打分；对于低凝聚频段，该项将保留完整的权重。

Equivalently expanded, this can be written as $\sum_f (\expected{\norm{q_f}} - \norm{\expected{q_f}}) \cdot \norm{k_f}$. For highly concentrated bands, $1 - R_f \to 0$ makes it barely participate in scoring; for low concentration bands, this term retains its full weight.

综合最终注意力预测打分 ($\tilde{S}$)

Comprehensive Final Attention Prediction Score ($\tilde{S}$)

两项组合为自适应联合评分，作为物理剔除的历史关键度评测机制：

The combination of both terms yields an adaptive joint score, serving as the historical criticality evaluation mechanism for physical eviction:

$$\tilde{S}(k, \Delta) = S_{trig}(k, \Delta) + S_{norm}(k)$$

💡 Insight: 通过此模型，具有强中心偏好偏向（RoPE 决定）的层使用精确的余弦旋转代数投影打分；而分布散乱的层则退化为按预期 Query-Norm 加权的 Key-Norm。边界条件完美无缝契合。

💡 Insight: Through this model, layers with strong center-preference biases (dictated by RoPE) utilize precise cosine rotational algebraic projection for scoring; whereas scattered layers degrade to Key-Norms weighted by expected Query-Norms. Boundary conditions fit seamlessly.

4. 在 vLLM 生态中的工程落地 4. Engineering Deployment in the vLLM Ecosystem

在 vLLM 中，TriAttention 作为非侵入式的运行时 KV 压缩插件注入，在无需改动任何模型结构的情况下，直接接入 PagedAttention 的物理内存块管理。

In vLLM, TriAttention is injected as a non-intrusive runtime KV compression plugin. It directly hooks into PagedAttention's physical memory block management without altering any underlying model structures.

vLLM 运行时动态压缩与内存回收工作流

vLLM Runtime Dynamic Compression & Memory Reclaim Workflow

拦截器挂钩与猴子补丁逻辑 Interceptor Hooks and Monkeypatch Logic

服务初始化时，执行以下脚本即可无缝拦截 vLLM 执行树：

Upon service initialization, executing the following script seamlessly intercepts the vLLM execution tree:

❶ 调度器拦截 (Scheduler Patch) ❶ Scheduler Patch

覆写 vLLM 的 BlockSpaceManager 块分配管理器，动态监控生成物理 Token 的序列步长。一旦步长触及阈值 DIVIDE_LENGTH（默认 128），即向底层执行引擎发送压缩显卡指令。

Overwrites vLLM's BlockSpaceManager block allocator to dynamically monitor the sequence step size of generated physical Tokens. Once the step size hits the DIVIDE_LENGTH threshold (default 128), it issues a GPU compression command to the underlying execution engine.

❷ 执行器拦截 (Worker Patch) ❷ Worker Patch

重写自注意力前向前置层。在自注意力内核执行前，它首先获取标定物理期望中心与当前 Key 的偏差角度（在 Pre-RoPE 坐标系），进而计算得到保留掩码，并直接修改物理 Key / Value 指针，剥离无意义项。

Rewrites the self-attention forward pre-layer. Before the self-attention kernel executes, it calculates the deviation angle between the calibrated physical expected center and the current Key (in the Pre-RoPE coordinate system), derives a retention mask, and directly modifies physical Key/Value pointers to strip out meaningless items.

物理压缩与页面级内存回收 Physical Compaction and Page-Level Memory Reclamation

仅对指针进行掩码拦截会导致空闲物理碎片残留在显存，必须彻底回收方可规避推理 OOM：

Merely performing mask interception on pointers leaves idle physical fragments residing in VRAM. Complete reclamation is mandatory to avoid inference OOMs:

❶ 原位物理重组 (In-place GPU Compaction) ❶ In-place GPU Compaction

集成高度优化的 Triton / HIP Gather 内核。它在 GPU 原位物理段执行高速“往前挪移挤压”，无须开辟临时的影子镜像缓冲区（Shadow Buffer），即可将分散残留的活跃 KV 数据在物理块内紧密排布。

Integrates a highly optimized Triton / HIP Gather kernel. It performs high-speed "forward shifting and squeezing" directly in GPU physical segments without creating temporary Shadow Buffers, tightly packing the dispersed active KV data within physical blocks.

❷ 页面级块回收 (Block Page Reclamation) ❷ Block Page Reclamation

在 PagedAttention 体系中，内存按每块 16 / 32 粒度的页面划分。一旦经过压缩，某整块逻辑分配页面的 16 个 Token 均被淘汰，回收钩子强制将其在 BlockSpaceManager 处的引用计数清零，并将物理地址扔回空闲队列以供重新分配。

In the PagedAttention system, memory is divided into page blocks of 16/32 granularity. Once a logically allocated block has all its tokens evicted post-compression, the reclamation hook forces its reference count in BlockSpaceManager to zero, returning the physical address to the free queue for reallocation.

5. 在 SGLang 框架中的高吞吐设计 5. High Throughput Design in SGLang Framework

2026 年 4 月 21 日发布的 TriAttention v0.2.0 中，官方正式将 SGLang 作为第一等公民进行深度对接。SGLang 自身高度追求极限吞吐与算子融合，对其落地提出了特定的工程需求。

In TriAttention v0.2.0 released on April 21, 2026, SGLang was officially embraced as a first-class citizen for deep integration. SGLang's intense pursuit of extreme throughput and operator fusion posed specific engineering requirements for its deployment.

TP

TP-Aware 统计参数多卡分片

TP-Aware Stats Parameter Multi-GPU Sharding

在多卡张量并行 (Tensor Parallel, TP) 状态下，自注意力头的计算会被物理切分。SGLang 端的 TriAttention 会根据 Rank ID 自动将加载的 model_stats.pt 进行切片分装，每张卡仅持有和加载属于自己卡对应 Head 的 Q-Center 与 Q-Norm，从而规避了分布式环境下的跨卡 All-Reduce 全局通信，实现完全并行的多卡高频推理。

Under Tensor Parallel (TP) conditions, self-attention head calculations are physically partitioned. TriAttention on SGLang automatically slices the loaded model_stats.pt based on Rank IDs, ensuring each GPU only holds and loads Q-Centers and Q-Norms for its corresponding heads. This avoids cross-GPU All-Reduce global communications in distributed environments, enabling fully parallel, high-frequency multi-GPU inference.

PH

基于 Head 级别的细粒度物理压缩

Fine-Grained Physical Compression at Head-Level

与粗暴统一剔除 Token 的粗放方式不同，不同注意力头对于同一个物理 Token 具有天差地别的相位距离和语义偏好。SGLang 联动高度定制的 sgl_kernel，在细粒度的 Per-Head 维度维持和推进异构非连续内存布局，最大程度护航数学推理上的熵信息完整性。

Unlike the crude approach of uniform Token eviction, different attention heads possess vastly different phase distances and semantic preferences for the same physical Token. SGLang, in conjunction with the highly customized sgl_kernel, maintains and advances heterogeneous, non-contiguous memory layouts at the fine-grained Per-Head dimension, maximizing the preservation of entropy integrity in mathematical reasoning.

RC

Radix Cache 前缀树缓存隔离

Radix Cache Prefix Tree Isolation

SGLang 标志性的核心技术为 Radix Cache。然而，由于动态 KV Cache 压缩在解码中由于对各历史 Token 掩码并不一致，会使前缀物理快散落重构并导致缓存树失真失效。为了维持系统稳定，SGLang 启动器的 TriAttention 默认会强行注入 --disable-radix-cache 命令以隔离树逻辑干扰。

SGLang's iconic core technology is Radix Cache. However, because dynamic KV Cache compression applies inconsistent historical Token masks during decoding, it scatters and reconstructs prefix physical blocks, causing the cache tree to distort and fail. To maintain system stability, TriAttention's SGLang launcher defaults to forcefully injecting the --disable-radix-cache command to isolate tree logic interference.

㈢ 頭級平均計算 (Head-level Aggregation) ㈢ Head-level Aggregation

按自注意力頭 (Head) 對逆向得到的坐標點計算中心均值 $\expected{q_f}$ 與期望模長，歸一化打包保存至 .pt 中。

Calculates the center mean $\expected{q_f}$ and expected norm of inverted coordinate points per Head, normalizing and packing them into the `.pt` file.
